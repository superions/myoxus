# Code of Conduct

Myoxus is a collaborative project. We want participation to be safe,
constructive, and focused on the work.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and assume good faith while remaining precise;
- focus criticism on ideas, code, documentation, and observable behavior;
- welcome different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints;
- give and receive actionable feedback;
- protect private information, credentials, and security-report details;
- follow the project's licensing, provenance, and contribution requirements.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following is not acceptable:

- harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, or sustained hostility;
- sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention;
- doxxing, publishing private information, or exposing credentials;
- deliberate disruption, spam, impersonation, or bad-faith reporting;
- submitting code or assets while concealing incompatible licensing or ownership;
- attempting to bypass security controls or disclose a vulnerability publicly
  before coordinated handling.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests,
chat spaces, meetings, release discussions, and other spaces where a person is
representing or materially participating in Myoxus.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers at
[myoxus@blume29.de](mailto:myoxus@blume29.de). Do not use the public issue tracker for
sensitive allegations or security vulnerabilities. Include the relevant links,
approximate dates, witnesses where appropriate, and any immediate safety
concerns. Remove credentials and unrelated personal data.

Maintainers will acknowledge reports, keep them confidential where practical,
assess conflicts of interest, and take proportionate action. They may request
additional information, remove content, restrict participation, or involve
other responsible parties when necessary.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may apply actions proportionate to the circumstances, including a
private warning, temporary restriction, removal of content, or permanent
exclusion from project spaces. Retaliation against a good-faith report is not
accepted.

Maintainers who are involved in a report must not decide its outcome. Appeals
and enforcement details will be documented in the project's support policy once
that policy is published.

## Attribution

This policy is inspired by established open-source community conduct standards
and is adapted for the Myoxus project.
